Bose has unveiled the **Noise Cancelling Wired Earbuds**, its first wired earbuds in eleven years, a product that brings back the physical connection without giving up active noise cancellation. The model arrives with a USB-C plug, the brand's QuietControl technology and a price of $99 in the United States, well below its wireless earbuds with ANC.

## Bose's return to wired earbuds

The announcement lands at a time when wired earbuds and IEMs are gaining ground again among listeners who prioritise low latency, uncompressed audio instead of Bluetooth and not having to rely on a battery. Bose is not abandoning wireless — just days ago it introduced its Ultra Open Earbuds 2 and Sport Open Earbuds — but it is reviving a category it had left untouched for more than a decade.

## QuietControl: noise cancellation with three modes

What stands out about wired earbuds is that they include active noise cancellation, and Bose has adapted its **QuietControl** system to this category. The company uses four microphones — two per earbud — to monitor ambient sound and offers three listening modes switched from the cable itself:

- **Quiet mode**: the highest reduction of ambient noise.
- **Aware mode**: lets outside sound in so you stay aware of your surroundings.
- **ANC off**: conventional wired listening, without active cancellation.

Unlike other wired models, these Bose earbuds draw power directly from the device through USB-C, so they **need no battery of their own**: the noise-cancelling features work as long as they are plugged in.

## Controls on the cable and lossless sound

The 1.28-metre cable includes an **in-line remote** with buttons to adjust the volume, control playback, manage calls and switch between the cancellation modes. That same module houses a microphone on its rear, positioned to reduce clothing noise and keep your voice clear on calls.

On the audio side, Bose turns to its **SoundDesign** digital signal processor with support for USB Audio Class 2, which enables lossless playback with a fixed output of **24-bit/48 kHz**. Because it is a physical connection, latency drops noticeably, something the brand highlights for video, gaming and content creation.

## Price, colours and availability

The Bose Noise Cancelling Wired Earbuds go on sale on **15 October 2026** for **$99** in the United States; there is still no confirmed price or date for Europe. They come in three finishes: black with blue accents, white smoke with lime-green accents and the limited **Dewdrop Mint** edition, a nostalgic nod.

The package includes silicone eartips in three sizes (S, M and L) and stability bands in two sizes, which can be combined for a better fit, plus a zip carry case. The whole set carries an **IPX4 rating** against splashes and sweat.

## What changes for the reader

Bose's bet is interesting because it breaks the idea that a cable and noise cancellation are incompatible: anyone who wants the reliability of a physical connection no longer has to give up the silence of ANC. At $99, the price also sits far from the company's premium wireless earbuds.

The European price remains to be seen, and whether the model delivers on sound quality, but the move fits a broader trend in which manufacturers are bringing back wired audio while continuing to push wireless platforms with advanced noise cancellation.
