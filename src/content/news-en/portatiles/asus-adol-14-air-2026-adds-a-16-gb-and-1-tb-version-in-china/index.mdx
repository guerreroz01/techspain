**The ASUS adol 14 Air 2026 is adding a configuration with 16 GB of RAM and a 1 TB SSD in China, according to MyDrivers.** The outlet puts its launch price at 5,949 yuan with subsidies included. The news is an expansion of configurations for this lightweight laptop, not the introduction of an entirely new chassis or its arrival in Spain.

## ASUS adol 14 Air 2026: 14-inch OLED and under a kilogram, according to the report

MyDrivers describes a 14-inch OLED screen with 2.8K resolution and a 120 Hz refresh rate. It also attributes a weight of 990 grams and a thickness of 14.9 mm to the device. These are specifications reported by that publication, not measurements performed by TechSpain24.

![ASUS adol screen displaying a Paris scene and planets in a promotional image](./assets/detail-1.jpg)

For someone carrying a computer every day, weight is a more useful reference than an unconditional performance promise. Even so, it does not by itself explain keyboard comfort, noise under load or thermal behavior, aspects requiring a test of the device.

The combination of mobility and OLED also appears in other devices from the brand, such as the [ASUS ZenBook 14 with Snapdragon X](/noticias/asus-zenbook-14-snapdragon-review/). It is not, however, the same model or configuration.

## The change is the memory and storage combination

The configuration described combines **16 GB of RAM with a 1 TB SSD**, an additional option within the adol 14 Air 2026 range. The report also mentions 32 GB configurations, but that does not mean all their prices and conditions apply to the entry model.

![Illustration of an AMD Ryzen AI processor included by MyDrivers](./assets/detail-2.jpg)

The 5,949-yuan amount refers to an offer with subsidies in the Chinese market. It is not a Spanish retail price or sufficient grounds to convert it into euros and present it as an import cost: the commercial conditions are not equivalent.

## What remains unconfirmed outside China

The information consulted does not establish a Spanish sale date, European distribution or local pricing. We have also been unable to verify the new configuration in a readable primary ASUS specification, so the scope of this news is limited to the MyDrivers report.

![Laptop presentation image included in the MyDrivers report](./assets/detail-3.jpg)

Promotional battery-life figures are not presented here as real-world results. For someone seeking a lightweight OLED laptop, the configuration is a development to follow; for a purchase decision in Spain, availability, warranty conditions and an independent assessment are still missing.
