Dune: Awakening is no longer just a PC phenomenon. Funcom's survival MMO arrived on PS5 and Xbox on September 22 and, only days later, the studio confirms it has added **one million new players**. The announcement came alongside the biggest patch the game has received so far, a move Funcom framed as a relaunch across every platform at once.

## One million new players in Dune: Awakening

The figure was made public by the Funcom team itself in a press release picked up by KitGuru. In their words, since the console launch and the major update, "one million new survivors have made Arrakis their home." It is no small number for a title that started on PC last year and, even then, sold over one million copies within its first two weeks.

The console push also comes with an added draw: the debut ships with a single-player mode that broadens the offer beyond the persistent server and shared survival.

## The caveat worth keeping in mind

There is an important difference between adding players and selling copies, and here the context itself invites a calm reading of the number. Dune: Awakening joined [Xbox Game Pass on its launch day](/noticias/xbox-game-pass-day-one-denial/), so a good share of that million may be subscription players who never bought the game separately.

On top of that, the most ambitious update to date rolled out across all platforms at once, PC included. Funcom does not break down how many of those new players came through consoles and how many returned for the patch, so treating the million as a PS5 and Xbox sales metric would be a sloppy shortcut.

## What changes for the player

For anyone who has not yet set foot on Arrakis, the timing is reasonable: the game is 50% off on Steam and the current console generation already supports the full catalogue with the new single-player mode. The question that matters is not how many players arrived, but how many stay: in a survival MMO, support across the generation and a steady flow of content weigh more than any launch spike.

Funcom has proven it can keep the game alive on PC for more than a year; the console test starts now, and the million arrivals are only the first chapter of the number worth tracking.
