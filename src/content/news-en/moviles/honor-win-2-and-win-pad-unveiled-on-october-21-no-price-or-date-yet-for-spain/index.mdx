# Honor Win 2 and Win Pad Unveiled on October 21, No Price or Date Yet for Spain

Honor has already set a date for its next gaming offensive: the **October 21**. On that day, during the Honor Win Gaming Universe Carnival event, the company will present the **Honor Win 2** series and the **Honor Win Pad**, its first tablet in the Win family. What is still missing is price, closed technical specs, or even a single word about whether either of them will leave China.

That is what matters most to those looking from Spain: for now, everything points to a domestic launch.

## The Win 2, Second Round for the Win Brand

It's not the first time Honor has used the surname Win. The firm launched the **Honor Win and Win RT in China in December 2025**, featuring an 185 Hz screen, a 10,000 mAh battery, and an integrated cooling fan. The Win 2 series takes over from that first generation.

Honor has already shown how the new phones will look: a horizontal camera module on the back with two sensors, alongside a fan and an RGB lighting block. The back also adds a pattern imitating clouds. The company has not yet revealed the specifications of the series.

The Win 2s will go on sale **on the same day as the presentation**, according to Honor, which markets them as "the high-end gaming device of the year".

## The Win Pad, Active Cooling in a Tablet

The other novelty at the event is the **Honor Win Pad**, the first tablet in the Win line to feature an integrated fan. In other words, active cooling in a format where passive dissipation is usually relied upon.

![Official render of the Honor Win Pad](./assets/win-pad.jpg)

Leaks suggest the tablet will carry a **Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5**, an OLED screen of **8.4 inches** and **240 Hz** refresh rate. Honor has not confirmed any of that data, so treat them as rumors until October 21. Reservations for the Win Pad will begin during the event itself.

## What Remains to Be Confirmed

Honor still needs to close the fine print on both devices: processors, battery capabilities, prices, and configurations. It has also not said whether the Win 2 series or the Win Pad will reach markets outside China, where the Win family has moved so far.

## Buying Verdict

Without a price and without an arrival date for Spain, there is nothing to decide yet. Anyone looking for a gaming phone will have to wait until October 21 and, above all, for Honor to confirm whether these devices cross the border. If the Win Pad maintains active cooling and comes at a contained price, it will be an interesting proposal; if it stays in China, it will be another piece to read with envy, not to buy.
