Logitech has announced the Zone Vibe Pro, a pair of wireless over-ear headphones the company pitches as an all-day model designed to cover both work calls and casual listening. The manufacturer targets people who move between the two contexts and do not want to rely on two separate pairs, and they arrive with adaptive active noise cancellation (ANC), AI-assisted voice isolation, and parts the user can replace themselves.

## Boomless design and calls on the Zone Vibe Pro

Unlike the Zone Vibe 100, its more affordable sibling, which relied on a boom microphone that mutes when flipped up, the new Pro opts for a boomless design. To capture the voice without that physical arm, Logitech includes an AI-powered voice isolation system that the company says filters up to 93% of background noise.

Sound is handled by custom-tuned 40 mm dynamic drivers, alongside transparency and adaptive cancellation modes configured through the Logi Tune desktop and mobile apps. The move fits a broader industry trend: AI-backed audio, such as Qualcomm's [Snapdragon Sound Elite Gen 2 platform](/noticias/qualcomm-snapdragon-sound-elite-gen-2/).

## Battery life, charging, and repairability

Battery life is one of the model's selling points. Logitech claims up to 30 hours of continuous listening or 18 hours of talk time with active noise cancellation on, rising to 66 hours with ANC turned off. Charging runs over USB-C and, according to the company, a five-minute top-up delivers an hour of calls. The headphones can also work as a wired USB audio device while charging, which is handy for anyone back-to-backing meetings.

On durability, Logitech bets on replaceable parts: the earpads, headband straps, and batteries can be swapped by the user, a move meant to improve long-term repairability. Bluetooth multipoint pairing is included as well.

## Business version and price

Alongside the consumer version, Logitech sells the Zone Vibe Pro for Business, aimed at corporate environments. This variant adds certifications for Microsoft Teams (with Open Office microphone standards), Zoom, and Google Meet, plus remote fleet management through Logitech Sync.

The standard version is available worldwide for $259.99 in four colors: graphite, black, off-white, and lilac. The Business Native Bluetooth Edition costs the same, and there is a bundle with a dedicated Zone USB-C wireless receiver for $279.99.
